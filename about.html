About Us

Promoting mutual understanding, defending rights, and empowering Arab & Muslim communities globally.

Who We Are

We are a leading organization committed to empowering Muslim and Arab communities in the United States and around the world. Through conscious, influential leadership and balanced international relations, AMAAC serves as an effective platform for civic participation, defending rights, and building cultural understanding.

Our Core Values

Justice

Upholding fairness, equity, and human rights for all.

Integrity

Operating with transparency, honesty, and accountability.

Compassion

Embodying empathy and care for all people.

Inclusivity

Celebrating diversity and ensuring all voices are heard.

Empowerment

Equipping communities with tools for self-determination.

Respect & Equality

Ensuring dignity and equality for all.

Our Mission

To promote mutual understanding, build bridges of dialogue, and advocate for the rights of Arab and Muslim communities in the United States and around the world.

Our Vision

To be a driving force for positive change, centered on promoting peace, justice, and mutual understanding between the Arab & Muslim world and America.

Key Pillars

Advocacy

Championing civil rights & social justice.

Community Empowerment

Fostering unity & leadership.

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating our rich heritage.
