Our Work

Addressing critical issues affecting Muslims and Arabs — locally and globally.

What We Do

AMAAC works across advocacy, policy, legal aid, media outreach, refugee support, and youth development to protect rights and strengthen communities.

Join Our Work

Civil Rights & Anti-Discrimination

We defend civil liberties and combat Islamophobia and anti-Arab discrimination through legal support, monitoring, and public education.

  • Legal clinics & referral networks for victims of discrimination.
  • Monitoring discriminatory practices in workplaces, schools, and law enforcement.
  • Public awareness campaigns to challenge bias and misinformation.
  • Know-Your-Rights workshops and resources.

Civic Engagement & Political Participation

We increase representation and empower communities to participate in civic life through voter education, leadership training, and policy advocacy.

Voter Education

Nonpartisan voter registration drives and informational workshops.

Leadership Programs

Training for emerging leaders to run for office and lead civic initiatives.

Media & Public Relations

We engage with media to promote fair coverage, produce educational content, and amplify voices that counter negative narratives.

  • Media training for community spokespeople.
  • Press outreach and rapid response to misinformation.
  • Production of articles, videos, and podcasts highlighting community stories.

Immigration & Refugee Support

We advocate for humane immigration policies and provide resources to support new arrivals and refugee families.

Resettlement Assistance

Partnerships with local NGOs to provide housing, legal, and social services.

Policy Advocacy

Campaigns and briefings that promote fair and humane immigration practices.

Human Rights & Foreign Policy

We raise awareness about global human rights concerns and advocate for U.S. foreign policies that promote peace, dignity, and justice.

  • Independent research & policy papers on regional issues.
  • Engagements with lawmakers and international bodies.
  • Humanitarian initiatives and rapid response programs.
