Our Work

Addressing critical issues affecting Muslims and Arabs in the USA and all over the world.

What We Do

AMAAC stands at the forefront of advocacy, working tirelessly to protect the civil rights and address the concerns of Muslims and Arabs. We engage with policymakers, educate the public, and mobilize our community to challenge discrimination, promote equitable policies, and ensure our voices are integral to the national dialogue. .

Join Our Work

Civil Rights & Anti-Discrimination

  • Combating Islamophobia and anti-Arab sentiment.
  • Addressing workplace and housing discrimination.
  • Protecting freedom of speech and religious expression.
  • Advocating against unlawful surveillance and profiling
  • Providing legal resources and support for victims of discrimination.

Human Rights & Foreign Policy

We raise awareness about global human rights concerns and advocate for U.S. foreign policies that promote peace, dignity, and justice.

  • Raising awareness about human rights issues affecting Muslim and Arab communities globally.
  • Advocating for U.S. foreign policies that promote peace, justice, and human dignity in the Middle East and beyond.
  • Supporting humanitarian efforts in regions facing conflict or crisis.
